Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to SportJersey ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our WhatsApp Business integration platform and related services (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform through Meta Embedded Signup and WhatsApp Cloud API, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID, Business Phone Number ID, Display Name, and Business Portfolio ID.
  • Authentication Data: OAuth authorization tokens necessary to communicate with the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API on your behalf. All authentication tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Messaging Data: Message metadata, timestamps, delivery statuses, and customer message contents routed through the WhatsApp Business API to fulfill automation and customer support requests.
  • Contact Information: Business email, phone number, and support inquiry details you provide to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To connect and authenticate your WhatsApp Business Account via Meta Embedded Signup.
  • To send, receive, and automate customer service messages and notifications requested by your business.
  • To maintain, secure, and monitor the performance of our integration service.
  • To comply with Meta Developer Platform policies and applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: As required to operate the WhatsApp Cloud API and Embedded Signup workflows.
  • Cloud Hosting Providers: Secure infrastructure providers (such as Render) that host our server applications under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. All Meta access tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption. Incoming webhook communications are verified using HMAC SHA-256 signatures.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request the deletion of your account and associated WhatsApp integration data at any time by contacting our support team or visiting our data deletion instructions page at Data Deletion Instructions.
