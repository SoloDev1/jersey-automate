User Data Deletion Instructions

SportJersey ("Jersey Automate") values your data privacy. In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and General Data Protection Regulations, users have the right to request the deletion of all data associated with their account and WhatsApp integration.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to remove your WhatsApp Business connection and delete all stored tokens, credentials, and message logs, you can do so through either of the following methods:

Method 1: Direct Disconnect via Dashboard

  1. Log in to your SportJersey dashboard.
  2. Navigate to the WhatsApp Integration section.
  3. Click the Disconnect WhatsApp button.
  4. This will immediately revoke all access tokens, disconnect webhook subscriptions, and wipe stored credentials from our database.

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to support@sportjersey.com with the subject line "WhatsApp Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID or registered phone number.
  • Your account name or registered email address.

Our team will process your request and permanently delete your data within 48 hours of verification.
